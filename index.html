A grassroots foundation for farming communities

Kiruba Foundation works in Oddanchatram, Dindigul district, on sustainable agriculture and farmer-focused development. Its work connects farm production, scientific waste management, organic inputs and farmer training.

Ensure equity, equality and social justice for the targeted communities under the MDG within the desired period of time.

Working together with the affected community, in close organisation, to work voluntarily for the overall comprehensive development, both socially and economically, of disadvantaged people, women and children. The organisation has devoted all its efforts, resources and manpower to ensure accepted standards in education and health by creating a favourable social and cultural environment.

Turning a vegetable-waste problem into organic fertilizer

In Oddanchatram, a NABARD impact story documents a partnership with Kiruba Foundation to handle large volumes of vegetable waste. The project introduced vermicomposting, scientific waste management and capacity building, so waste became organic fertilizer and farmers could adopt sustainable practices.

Part of a wider farmer and FPO network

The Tamil Nadu Department of Agricultural Marketing and Agri Business lists Kiruba Sustainable Agriculture Farmers Producer Company Limited, Oddanchatram, among vegetable-focused Farmer Producer Companies. NABARD is shown as the promoting agency.

Public professional profiles also record recent agricultural student attachments and field learning connected with the FPO and Kiruba Foundation.
